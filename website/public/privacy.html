Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

This policy explains what Team Code (“we”) collects when you use the TeamCode website, accounts and desktop app, and what we do with it. We collect as little as we can.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and a one-way hash of your password (scrypt). We never see or store your actual password.
  • Sign-in sessions: a hash of each session token, when it was created and last used, and the browser or app identifier (user agent), so you can see where you're signed in.
  • App sign-in codes: the short code used to sign in the desktop app. These expire after 10 minutes and are deleted once used.
  • Cloud projects: if you host a project on TeamCode cloud, we store its files, earlier versions of each file, tasks, and team activity (who changed what, and when) so your team can sync. Only people with the project's invite code can reach it.
  • IP addresses: used briefly in server memory for rate limiting and abuse prevention. We don't store them in our database. Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs.

What we don't collect

  • Code in projects hosted on your own computer. Those files sync between your team's computers through a workspace server your team runs and never reach our servers.
  • Your Claude Code conversations. Agents run on your own Claude Code, which talks directly to Anthropic under Anthropic's own privacy policy.
  • Analytics or advertising data. We don't use analytics, tracking pixels or advertising cookies.

Cookies

We set one cookie, a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It's strictly necessary and isn't used for tracking.

The desktop app

  • The app checks this website for updates when it starts and every few hours. The request includes your IP address and the app's identifier.
  • If you sign in, the app stores a sign-in token on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's keychain where available.

Services we rely on

  • Railway hosts this website, its database, and cloud project files.
  • GitHub stores the installers. Download links send you to GitHub to fetch the file.
  • Google Fonts serves the website's fonts, so Google receives your IP address when pages load.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours.

How long we keep it

Account details are kept until you delete your account. Cloud project files and activity are kept until you delete the project or your account. Website sessions expire after 30 days and app sessions after 90 days. When you delete your account, your details, sessions and cloud projects are deleted straight away; copies in our hosting provider's backups expire on their normal schedule.

Your rights

You can see your details and delete your account from the Account page. To ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or anything else about your privacy, email [contact email]. You may also complain to your local privacy regulator.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, session tokens are stored only as hashes, connections use HTTPS, and sign-in is rate limited with lockouts after repeated failures. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll tell you as the law requires.

Children

TeamCode isn't for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way, we'll tell you by email or in TeamCode before it takes effect.
